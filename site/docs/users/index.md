# BT Wallet

A wallet for [Epic Cash](https://epiccash.com), made by BT LABS. One app for:

- Windows and Linux, with a preview for macOS.
- Android, with a preview for iPhone and iPad.

The desktop app and the phone app use the same wallet core, the same addresses, the same words and
the same Help. A recovery phrase made in one opens the wallet in the other.

Up to 26.10.24 the app was called Epic Cash Desktop and Epic Cash Mobile.
[Install](install.md#from-epic-cash-desktop-or-epic-cash-mobile) says how to move to BT Wallet.

## What the wallet does

- Holds your EPIC. The keys stay on your device, locked with a password (desktop) or a PIN
  (phone).
- Sends and receives over **Epicbox**, the everyday way: the other wallet does not have to be
  online at the same time.
- Also sends straight to another open wallet (**Link · HTTP**, **Link · Tor**), by file
  (**Direct · File Transfer**), or phone to phone with an animated QR code
  (**Direct · QR Payment**).
- Connects to the Epic Cash network through a node: a public one by default, or your own.
- Optional Tor, on the desktop and on Android.
- [Pairs with contacts](pairing.md): sealed payments, files and invoices between two wallets.

## Where to start

1. [Install the app](install.md) for your device.
2. [Create your first wallet](first-wallet.md) and write down the

If something does not work, read [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Words you do not know are

:::note
